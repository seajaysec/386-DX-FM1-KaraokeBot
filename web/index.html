Songs on the FM-1

Eight slots. For each, choose what should happen: one of our songs or your own file put there, a download, or a delete. Then press Sync to do them all.

Import a song

A karaoke file (.kar) or a MIDI file (.mid) with its words in it as timed lyrics: each syllable at the note that sings it. The page finds the sung tune and turns the other parts into Keys, Bass and Drums. Words in plain English letters; spell numbers out.
